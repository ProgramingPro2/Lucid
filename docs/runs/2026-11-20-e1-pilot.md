## Why the pilot cannot be interpreted

Measured on this machine, not inferred.

1. **Budget.** The cached corpus for this grid is 3,883 documents / 16,404,421
   tokens (`artifacts/e1/cache/corpus_8e4ff3b729fed759.npz`). The document
   split (`test_fraction=0.1`, `split_seed=20260601`) puts 3,495 documents /
   **14,821,030 tokens** in train. 2,002,944 tokens seen is **0.135 epochs**
   of that train split. The convergence rule could not have been met, and the
   run is a short prefix of the budget the protocol describes.

2. **Four blockers, all verified before this note was written.**
   - **Causality.** `JEPABlock.forward` called `nn.MultiheadAttention` with
     only `key_padding_mask`. The inserted block let every position attend to
     future tokens. Changing token 20 of a 32-token toy batch moved logits at
     positions 16–19 by ~1.4e-3. The language-model loss is therefore leaky,
     and arms A/B/E/N all carry the block.
   - **Washout diagnostic is identically zero.** Every one of the 21 runs
     recorded `block_rms` 0.0 and `block_fraction` 0.0, including arms whose
     block is trained. `E1Model._hook` wrote those stats on every eval-mode
     forward, and `evaluate` ablates the block and re-forwards before reading
     them, so the zeroed contribution overwrote the real one. A null P3 cannot
     be told apart from a washed-out block (protocol §5.3).
   - **No reproducibility record and no checkpoints.** The on-disk manifest
     has eight fields (`layer_index`, `d_model`, `n_trainable`, `n_block`,
     `n_head`, `eval_fingerprint`, `train_docs`, `test_docs`). It does not
     have `run_id`, `git_commit`, `config_sha256`, `corpus_sha256`,
     `spawn_keys`, `token_budget`, `final_checkpoint`, `wall_clock_s`,
     `peak_vram_gb`, or `nan_skips`. No run called `torch.save` or
     `save_pretrained`. There is no checkpoint to re-evaluate.
   - **Sample-size helper.** `summarise` called `required_n(sd, mpid)` with
     the defaults (`pilot_inflation=1.0`, `floor=1`) and printed
     `required n at pilot SD: 1`. The paired SD it plugged in is a
     within-design variance, not a run-level variance, and the call did not
     apply the protocol §5.4 inflation, floor 24, or cap 64.

3. **D and PC_hi are the same experiment and do not agree.** Both arms are
   wired with no block, JEPA off, and learning rate 1e-4. At seed 0 their mean
   P1 over the same 64 items differs by **0.00287** (D 0.79557, PC_hi 0.79844).
   At seed 1 the gap is 0.00070. The seed-0 gap is larger than the A − B mean
   (+0.00046). A duplicate of the vehicle is not a stable baseline at this
   budget, so the positive control cannot be read either.

4. **JEPA loss sits on the identity floor.** An identity floor of **0.9092**
   was measured on this backbone, layer, and delta: the cosine loss of
   predicting a state's own future when the block contributes nothing. Arm A
   logged JEPA between 0.908 and 0.923 across seeds and steps (A seed 0:
   0.917, 0.918, 0.922, 0.909, 0.914). That is the floor, not a predictor that
   has moved. Arm E, whose targets are permuted across the batch, sits above
   the floor at about 1.02 (E seed 0: 1.019–1.026). Scrambled targets are
   harder than the identity, which is what an untrained block should do, and
   it is not evidence that the objective trained.

## Exclusion

All 21 runs are pilot-only and are excluded from analysis under the
reproducibility rule (docs/EXPERIMENTS.md §11, enforced by
`experiments.protocol.manifest`). They are also not a qualification pass:
protocol §10 requires zero sanitised microbatches, a recorded token-stream
hash, and finite eval metrics that mean what the arm claims. This grid cannot
show the sanitised-microbatch count, and the causal leak means the LM loss on
every block arm is not the loss the protocol specifies.

Do not quote A − B, the per-arm means, or `required n = 1` as evidence about
whether a JEPA block is load-bearing.
